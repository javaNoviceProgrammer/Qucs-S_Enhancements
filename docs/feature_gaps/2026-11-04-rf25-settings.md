# RF 2.5D > Settings…: the dense LU, the dense limit and a sweep's memory

The user asked, after the dense LU moved to Accelerate (9edbb9d, `2026-11-04-rf25-dense-lu.md`), for two choices that
had been left as constants to become settings under the RF 2.5D menu, "similar to how we set up the settings GUI for
the Multiphysics", with sensible defaults:

- **the dense size limit** (`kMostUnknowns`, 6000 runs and cells), past which the full wave holds its system
  compressed;
- **the memory a dense sweep's frequencies at once may hold** (`kSweepMemory`, 1.5 GB). It governs how many
  frequencies are solved at once.

A third setting came with them, as the Multiphysics dialog has its solver: **which library factorizes the dense
systems**.

## What it is

**RF 2.5D > Settings…** (`rf25settings.{h,cpp}`, `rf25SettingsAction`, no menu role, as Multiphysics' has, so that
macOS does not take it for the application's Preferences) has three settings:

| setting | key | choices | Automatic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dense LU | `RF25/lu` | Automatic, Eigen's LU, Accelerate's LAPACK where this macOS has it | Accelerate's LAPACK on macOS 13.3 and later, Eigen's elsewhere |
| Held dense up to | `RF25/denseLimit` | 0 (Automatic) to 30,000 runs and cells | 6000, as before |
| A sweep's frequencies at once may hold | `RF25/sweepMemory` | 0 (Automatic) to the machine's memory, GB | a quarter of the machine's memory, 1.5 GB at least (17.2 GB here) |

- **Where they live:** in `QucsSettingsFile`, like Multiphysics' `Multiphysics/solver`. They are given to the solver at
  the start (`main.cpp`, beside `applySolverSetting()`) and on OK; Cancel keeps nothing.
- **What the dialog says:** under each group, what is in effect. For the LU, Accelerate or Eigen, with
  `QUCS_RF25_LU` or `QUCS_FEM_SOLVER=eigen` named where the environment chooses, whatever the dialog says. For the
  size: the limit, the memory, and how many systems of the limit's size are solved at once (6000 here: some 321 MB
  each, 53 at a time in Automatic's 17.2 GB, the threads allowing).
- **The solver** reads them as each solve begins: `setDenseLimit()`/`denseLimit()` and
  `setSweepMemory()`/`sweepMemory()` in `rf25/rf25_fullwave.h`, beside `setDenseLu()` in `rf25/rf25_dense.h`. A
  `FullWaveControl`'s own `mostUnknowns` (the tests', the calibrations') is over the setting.
- **A frequency's memory**, from which the sweep reckons how many fit at once, is now the measured
  `16 (nr² + nc²) + 1e6` bytes: the system, factorized where it is, and P. The old `16 (2 nr² + nc²)` counted an LU
  copy that is no longer made. In the app it held: at a budget of 1.5, 3, 6 and 12 GB the coplanar line's sweep peaked
  at 1.8, 3.1, 6.1 and 10.7 GB, the base app and the parts made once per solve some half a gigabyte of it.
- **Texts:** the study's Matrix tooltip, `rf25_solve`'s description and its `matrix` argument now name the limit as
  set in RF 2.5D > Settings, not "6000". The description is 2046 characters, inside the 2048 cap. A dense solve refused
  past the limit says "or raise the limit (RF 2.5D > Settings…)".

## The defaults, measured

On an M2 Ultra, through `rf25_solve` in the app (driven headless, isolated folders, each solve in a fresh app), one
frequency at 10 GHz, Accelerate's LU. Other sessions were busy on the machine (load 8 to 39), and the two of each pair
ran in turn.

**The limit: dense against compressed past 6000**, the limit raised for the dense solve (minimum of two each):

| model | runs + cells | dense | compressed (GMRES iterations) | S11, dense − compressed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `fgc_S62_L1000_r15p6_c10` (coplanar, narrow gaps) | 6996 | 6.75 s | 7.72 s (180) | 1.0e-3 dB of -34.97 |
| `cp50_L1000_m10` | 7116 | 4.37 s | 0.60 s (29) | 4e-8 dB |
| `ms30_L500_m2.5` | 8268 | 5.47 s | 0.51 s (28) | 4e-9 dB |
| `ms300_L1000_m10` | 9658 | 8.52 s | 0.81 s (30) | 9e-8 dB |
| `fgc_S62_L2000_r0p1_c10` (coplanar) | 13176 | 21.1 s | 19.1 s (200) | 1.5e-5 dB |
| `ms100_L1000_m5` | 13108 | 13.6 s | 0.77 s (31) | 3e-8 dB |

- **For the microstrips, compressed wins.** Past 6000 it was 7 to 17 times quicker: GMRES converges in 28 to 31
  iterations, and the compressed matrix is some 34 to 79 MB where the dense system and P are 0.4 to 1.5 GB.
- **For a coplanar line of narrow gaps, the two are about even.** It takes 180 to 200 iterations; dense was 13 %
  quicker at 6996 and 10 % slower at 13176.
- **So Automatic stays at 6000**, with Accelerate's LU as with Eigen's. My estimate of the morning, "about 8500 costs
  no more time a frequency than 6000 did" (`2026-11-04-rf25-dense-lu.md`), measured the dense solve against itself.
  Against the compressed one, a higher limit pays only for a hard layout of that kind, and the setting is there for it.
  (Since 47798db graded full-wave meshes at narrow gaps, that coplanar model is 6996 unknowns, no longer 5388.)

**The memory: a dense sweep against what its frequencies at once may hold.** The same coplanar line, its own
40 frequencies, held dense at a limit of 7000. A frequency is some 435 MB at 6996 unknowns (minimum of two each):

| memory | at a time | sweep | the app's peak | a frequency's fill / LU on its thread |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.5 GB (the old fixed one) | 3 | 73.3 s | 1.8 GB | 4.0 / 1.1 s |
| 3 GB | 6 | 44.0 s | 3.1 GB | 4.1 / 1.8 s |
| 6 GB | 13 | 31.0 s | 6.1 GB | 5.4 / 3.3 s |
| 12 GB | 24 (the threads) | 26.5 s | 10.7 GB | 6.4 / 6.0 s |
| compressed, for reference | 24 | 77.2 s | 5.5 GB | 384 iterations at most |

- **More at once is quicker**, though each frequency is slower as more share the memory and the matrix units: 2.4
  times at 6 GB, 2.8 at 12.
- **Automatic is a quarter of the machine's memory, no less than 1.5 GB:** 16 GB on a 64 GB machine (17.2 here), 4 on a
  16 GB one, 2 on an 8 GB one. A sweep of the default limit's size needs at most the threads times 321 MB (7.7 GB on
  24 threads), so on a large machine the budget seldom binds, and on a small one it keeps the sweep to a quarter.

## Tests

- **`test_rf25_doc::theSettingsHaveTheLuTheLimitAndTheMemory`** walks the dialog from the menu:
  - the menu item, its text, and no role;
  - Automatic, all three, by default: their boxes reading "Automatic", and the status saying the limit (6000) and the
    memory (Automatic's) with how many of the limit's size go at once;
  - Eigen's LU said when chosen, Automatic's limit the same whichever LU;
  - Cancel keeping nothing; OK keeping all three and giving them to the solver (`denseLu()`, `denseLimit()`,
    `sweepMemory()`), with the status's exact count for 1500 and 2.5 GB (119 at a time, some 21 MB each);
  - given again at a start (`applySettings()`), and shown again by a new dialog;
  - `QUCS_RF25_LU=accelerate` and `QUCS_FEM_SOLVER=eigen` said over the choice;
  - all back to Automatic.
- **`test_rf25_fullwave::theDenseLimitAndTheSweepMemoryAreTheSettings`** checks the solver obeys them:
  - a limit of 200 holds a line compressed and says so, and a control's own limit is over it;
  - Automatic's limit again at nought;
  - four frequencies on eight threads go 2, 1 and 4 at a time as 2.5, 0.5 and Automatic's many of a frequency's
    16 (nr² + nc²) + 1e6 bytes allow;
  - Automatic's memory is a quarter of the machine's, 1.5 GB at least.
- **`whatItCannotSolveIsSaid`** reads the limit in effect ("(6000 in all)", the setting's) and the refusal's new words:
  "more than a dense complex matrix is set up for", "raise the limit".

## Breaks

14 were caught.**

The start's `applySettings()` in `main.cpp` has no test of its own. The headless app's runs above are its evidence: their
limit and memory came from the settings file at the start, the dense solves past 6000 and the frequencies at a time
following them (3, 6, 13, 24). The worktree's build was rebuilt after the last break.
