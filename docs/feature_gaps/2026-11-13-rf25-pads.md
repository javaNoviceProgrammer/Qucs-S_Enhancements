# RF 2.5D's GSG pads above 35 GHz, against Palace 3D

Phase 6 of the plan for RF 2.5D's accuracy limits. It covers two of the open items of 8 November:
- **A3:** loss read through GSG pad structures went wrong above about 35 GHz, wherever the probes were. A cut signal
  line still coupled the two ends along the grounds at −45 dB.
- **N2:** the benchmark line's de-embedded attenuation rippled above 60 GHz, and at 66 GHz the de-embedding gave
  power back.

**The answer:** what the pads lose above 35 GHz is the structure's own. Their ends radiate into the air and into the
quartz, which is open below, and Palace 3D shows the same radiation. L-2L through the pads then reads that
radiation as the line's attenuation. No solver code changed. N2 no longer happens on this branch.

## The structures

The scripts beside this write-up make each structure in both solvers.
- **The pads** (`make_rf25.py`): GSG pads with tapers onto the benchmark's coplanar line.
  - The line: S 50, G 20, grounds 160 µm, on 500 µm of quartz (εr 3.8), open below.
  - The pads: B4's, a signal pad 80 µm wide and 60 long, an 80 µm taper, and ground pads from 70 µm out.
  - Lines of 500 and 1000 µm between them.
  - Metal either perfect, or 2 µm of gold (σ 4.1e7) solved as Automatic, which here is two faces.
- **RF 2.5D's probes:** an internal port on the signal pad's first 10 µm at each end, returning through the ground
  pads beside it.
- **Palace's model** (`make_palace.py`): the same layout in 3D, as a half model with a magnetic wall on the line's
  axis.
  - The box: a first-order absorbing boundary 600 µm past the pads' ends and 1000 µm out from the axis, with 1000 µm
    of air above and below.
  - The ports: at each end, a lumped port across the gap between the signal pad and its ground pad, on the same
    first 10 µm (100 Ω in the half, 50 Ω whole).
  - The metal: perfect metal as sheets inside the domain. Gold is cut out of the mesh, with a conductivity boundary
    on its faces, as the benchmark's comparison 1 did.
  - The solve: order 2 with Palace's adaptive driven sweep, 1–100 GHz.
- **Palace's runs:** one at a time, 8 ranks, only while no build or test run was going (`chain.sh`).
  - The linear solve is AMS-preconditioned GMRES. Palace's default, a sparse direct coarse solve, peaked at 29 GB on
    the smallest model; AMS took 20 GB and agreed with it to 8e-7 in |S|.
  - The models came to 2.3–4.1 million unknowns, 20–37 GB, and 15–57 minutes each. A watchdog would have stopped a
    run if available memory fell under 6 GB; none came near it.

## Perfect metal: the radiation is the same

RF 2.5D at 3 and at 6 across differ by little; these are 6 across. Lost means the power lost, 1 − |S11|² − |S21|².

| GHz | 500 µm lost, RF 2.5D / Palace | 1000 µm lost, RF 2.5D / Palace | L-2L α (dB/mm), RF 2.5D / Palace |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | 0.00006 / 0.00019 | 0.00039 / 0.00071 | 0.003 / 0.005 |
| 50 | 0.00025 / 0.00044 | 0.00203 / 0.00263 | 0.020 / 0.025 |
| 60 | 0.00082 / 0.00111 | 0.00597 / 0.00675 | 0.064 / 0.070 |
| 70 | 0.00233 / 0.00290 | 0.00563 / 0.00562 | 0.049 / 0.039 |
| 80 | 0.00573 / 0.00698 | 0.00348 / 0.00376 | 0.014 / 0.032 |
| 90 | 0.01108 / 0.01165 | 0.00232 / 0.00310 | 0.094 / 0.101 |
| 100 | 0.01347 / 0.01278 | 0.00258 / 0.00374 | 0.132 / 0.116 |

- **The lost power** is within 0.006 dB of Palace's at every frequency and on both lengths. The plan asked for
  0.02 dB.
- **Where it goes:** nothing in these structures dissipates, so what is lost is radiated.
- **The two lengths interfere differently:** the 500 µm structure loses more the higher the frequency, the 1000 µm
  one most at 60–70 GHz. Both solvers show it.
- **L-2L through the pads** reads an attenuation where the line has none. It rises at 60 GHz, falls at 80 and rises
  again at 100, in both solvers. The ends of the two lengths radiate and couple differently, so they are not the same
  ends, and L-2L cannot cancel them.
- **The slab's own waves aren't the cause:** the quartz's TM0 surface wave stays faster than the line (εeff 1.22 at
  100 GHz against the CPW's 2.36), and TE1 starts only at 179 GHz.
- **S11 and the phase differ:** S11 by up to 4.7 dB at 100 GHz, and Palace's S21 phase runs 4.7–5.2 % ahead at every
  frequency. A constant ratio like that is the two port models, a gap port against a source with returns, about
  10 pH apart. L-2L takes it out: εeff 2.400 against Palace's 2.410.

## Gold: Palace's metal loss is no reference

On 2 µm gold, RF 2.5D's 500 µm structure loses 12–23 % more than Palace's below 50 GHz, and 1–3 % more above 80.
The difference is in the metal, not the pads:
- **Two solvers that resolve the metal's inside** agree on the line's attenuation: eddy.py's 2D eddy-current solve
  and QFEM's. RF 2.5D agrees with them; Palace's conductivity boundary is low, in its fine 2D mode solve as much as in
  3D.

  | GHz | eddy.py | QFEM | RF 2.5D, L-2L through the pads | Palace 2D | Palace 3D (comparison 1) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 28.0 | 27.7 | | 27.3 | |
  | 10 | 55.3 | 55.3 | 55.0 | 41.4 | 41.8 |
  | 30 | 94.9 | 95.1 | 95.5 | 75.9 | 75.9 |
  | 60 | 136.7 | 137.7 | | 115.0 | 115.0 |

  The table is in dB/m. RF 2.5D's 30 GHz figure is less the perfect metal's reading, 0.4 dB/m.
- **The pads' gold loss** with Palace's metal deficit taken out is within 4–9 % of Palace's, and the radiation is the
  same in both. Palace's deficit is its 2D line's attenuation over QFEM's: 0.75 at 10 GHz, 0.80 at 30, 0.83 at 50.

Palace's gold L-2L through the pads has the same hump as RF 2.5D's: 0.193 against 0.199 dB/mm at 60 GHz, 0.175
against 0.195 at 70. Below it, Palace reads its own lower metal loss: 43.6 against 55.0 dB/m at 10 GHz. Palace's
1000 µm gold model was meshed at 3 µm along the metal's edges, against 2 µm for the 500 µm one, so that it would fit
in memory; it peaked at 35.6 GB.

The plan asked for the pads' gold loss within 10 % of Palace's from 35 to 67 GHz. RF 2.5D is 10–16 % over there, all
of it Palace's metal model, so the test holds RF 2.5D to the 2D references instead.

## N2: the benchmark line's de-embedding

The benchmark's calibrated 500 µm line, solved on this branch:
- **Passive** at 6 and 12 across, from 2 to 100 GHz, with no warning.
- **α is smooth:** 130–136 dB/m from 50 to 84 GHz, the same at 6 and 12 across. The ripple of 116–238 dB/m reported
  at 12 across is gone.
- **Above 86 GHz** the calibrated α leaves the line's own uncalibrated L-2L figure (500 against 1000 µm): 186 against
  168 dB/m at 100 GHz. All three of the calibration's strips (0.82, 1.33, 1.64 mm) read 186.6 dB/m there and agree
  within 0.2 %. Their ends radiate as the pads' do, and longer pieces of line read more. This is the same family as
  the pads, and it is left open.

## Checked

- **`test_rf25_accuracy`** `theRadiationOfGsgPadsIsPalaces` (new):
  - the perfect-metal pads at 500 and 1000 µm, at 3 across, 40–100 GHz;
  - the lost power within 0.02 dB of Palace's numbers, stored in the test, at both lengths;
  - L-2L's α within 0.025 dB/mm of Palace's;
  - the two lengths' and L-2L's swings as Palace's;
  - passive and reciprocal. It takes 0.3 s.
- **`aLineThroughGsgPadsLosesWhatItsCrossSectionDoes`** (new): the gold pads at 10–40 GHz. L-2L through them, less
  the perfect metal's reading, is within 3 % of eddy.py's attenuation (measured −0.6, 0.0, +0.6 and +0.8 %).
- Both pass under ASan and UBSan, in 61 s together; `test_rf25_accuracy` passes under ctest and the four lints pass.
- **`breaks.py`** undoes the physics the new tests stand on. Its results are in `results.txt` beside it.

## Not done

- **The calibration above 86 GHz** follows its strips' radiation, which a 500 µm line has less of.
- **A gold reference in 3D** would need a solver that resolves the metal's inside, or a finer metal model in Palace.
