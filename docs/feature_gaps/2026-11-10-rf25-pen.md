# The pen on RF 2.5D's tab

The request: "Add the pen marker to the RF 2.5D panel as well so that I can draw stuff on the open panel for claude to look
at and understand", with a picture of the tab's toolbar and an arrow at its right end.

## What was built

- **The pen** is at the right end of the RF 2.5D tab's toolbar, after the layout's line, where the picture pointed. It is
  the layout tab's pen, the same button and menu: pen, highlighter or eraser, a colour or any other, how broad, Undo the
  Last Change, and clearing the drawings of the cell shown or all of them. The pen in hand is every viewer's, as before.
- **Taken up**, a drag over the layout draws on its cell. Strokes pan and zoom with the layout, over the processed shapes,
  the mesh, the currents, the ports and the Cutout's regions. Escape puts the pen down, and Ctrl+Z undoes the last stroke,
  as on a layout's tab: the view is the layout tab's own view.
- **The pen and the port tools are one at a time.** Taking up the pen puts down Edge Port or Internal Port. Taking up either
  of them puts down the pen.

## What reaches Claude

- **Each prompt** from the Claude Code panel about the model brings what was drawn since the last one, a sentence a stroke,
  as for a layout: "cell TOP, stroke 1: a … pen line (its box … µm) …". It says too when the pen is still taken up, and that
  show_layout's `pen: false` puts it down. When the strokes are taken away, the next prompt says so.
- **read_drawings** reads a model's strokes, by its file or every open one, in µm of the cell, y up. They are read against
  the layout's shapes by GDSII layer, as on a layout's tab.
- **show_layout** on a model takes `pen`, `drawings: "clear"` and `addressed`, as on a layout's tab. Its answer has the
  strokes read, and says whether the pen is taken up. `cell`, `layers`, `depth`, `overlay`, `select`, `rulers` and the
  look's arguments are still refused for a model.
- **send_input**'s target `layout` draws on the model in front, or the one `path` names, when no layout's tab is meant.

## Not done

- The strokes are read against the layout file's layers ("1/0"), not the stack's names the layer map gives them ("M1").
- Claude's own `overlay` is still only a layout tab's.
- Like a layout's, the strokes are kept while the tab is open, not saved with the model.

## Tests

- `test_qucs_control`'s `anRf25ModelsPenIsReadThroughTheTools`:
  - the pen at the toolbar's end;
  - taken up and put down by show_layout;
  - a line drawn by send_input;
  - the strokes read by show_layout, read_drawings and a prompt's note, and the note not given twice;
  - a stroke marked addressed, and one it does not have refused;
  - the pen and the Internal Port tool one at a time;
  - the menu's Undo;
  - the drawings cleared, and the next prompt told so.

[breaks.py](2026-11-10-rf25-pen/breaks.py) undoes each piece on purpose, builds and runs the test. All 12 breaks are
caught: [results.txt](2026-11-10-rf25-pen/results.txt).

The first full run also caught two things in the wording, not in the code. read_drawings' refusal of a document no pen
draws on lost the phrase test_pptx_doc looks for, and show_layout's description went past the 2,048 characters a tool's
description may have. Both are fixed.
