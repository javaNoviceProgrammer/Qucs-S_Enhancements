# The pen on a PDF's tab

The request: "Add the pen marker panel that we added to layout viewer and other viewers also to PDF viewer. Add its button
on the right side "Digitize" button", with a picture of the PDF tab's toolbar and an arrow at Digitize.

## What was built

- **The pen** is the next button right of Digitize, before Find. It is the other viewers' pen, the same button and menu:
  pen, highlighter or eraser, a colour or any other, how broad, Undo the Last Change, and clearing the drawings of the page
  in sight or of all of them. The pen in hand is every viewer's, as before.
- **Taken up**, a drag over a page draws on it, before the selection, the links and the digitizer's tools take the
  button: with the pen up a drag draws rather than selects text, and a double click is two presses of the pen, not a word
  selected. A stroke goes on past its page's edge, drawn as far as the edge. Beside the pages a press does nothing.
- **Escape** lets go of a stroke being drawn, then puts the pen down. **Ctrl+Z** with the pen up undoes the last change
  to the drawing (the window's Undo is off while a PDF is in front, and the view claims the keys before the window's
  shortcuts). The digitizer's Ctrl+Z is its own again once the pen is down.
- **Strokes are held in points** from the page's top left, on the page's number from 1 (`PageView::partOf`), so they pan
  and zoom with the page. They are drawn over everything, the digitizer's marks too, as an image of what of them is in
  sight: the GPU's surface (`GpuSurface`) has fills and images only, and a page at 800% is not one image.
- **Kept** when the file is read again; not saved, printed, exported or digitized: they are not part of the document.

## What reaches Claude

- **Each prompt** from the Claude Code panel brings what was drawn since the last one (`QucsApp::drawingsOf`), a sentence a
  stroke, as for a presentation's slides: 'page 1, stroke 2: a #00a000 pen ring around "Hello" in the text "Page 1. Hello
  world, see the" (its box … points)' (the line is read as far as the link after it). Each stroke is read against the page's words about it: the digitizer's
  `Source::tokens` on the lines from 40 points above the strokes to 40 below, across the page, a line of them a target
  (`markup::readPage`), with its words. So a ring says what it is round, an underline what it
  underlines, an arrow what it points at.
- **Not done:** no tool reads a PDF's strokes apart (`read_drawings` covers layouts, slides, schematics and data
  displays), takes its pen up or down, marks strokes addressed, or draws on it through `send_input`. The prompt's note is
  what Claude has. So the note says nothing of the pen still being up, as the others do with their tool's `pen: false`,
  and past 30 strokes says the earlier ones were left out without pointing at `read_drawings`. Adding the PDF to those
  tools means the shared tool table (`qucscontrol.cpp`), whose descriptions are capped.

## Files

- `qucs/pdfdoc.h`, `qucs/pdfdoc.cpp`: `PageView`'s pen (taken up, drawing, erasing, Escape and Ctrl+Z, the strokes painted
  per page in sight); `PdfDoc`'s pen button, its menu's undo and clearing, `readDrawing` and `drawingSentences`.
- `qucs/qucs.cpp`: `QucsApp::drawingsOf` tells a PDF's strokes.
- `qucs/tests/test_pdf_viewer.cpp`: `thePenDrawsOnThePages`.

## Tests

- **New, `thePenDrawsOnThePages`:**
  - The button sits right after Digitize in the toolbar.
  - A drag with the pen up makes one stroke on page 1, at the points dragged through and as broad as the pen, with no
    text selected. It shows on screen where it was drawn, and not 20 points beside it.
  - `drawingsOf` tells "page 1" once a revision. A ring round "Hello" reads as round it, in `readDrawing` and in the note,
    with its line whole: taken 40 points about the strokes alone, the line was cut mid-word ("…Hello world, se").
  - Ctrl+Z takes the ring back. Escape puts the pen down. With the pen down Ctrl+Z is not the pen's.
  - The eraser crossing the line takes it away, and Ctrl+Z gives it back.
  - The strokes are kept through a reload.
- **Broken on purpose,** each caught by it: the pen not first on a press; the strokes not painted; `drawingsOf` without
  the PDF; the words not given as targets; Escape not putting the pen down.
- **Through ctest,** Release: `test_pdf_viewer`, `test_plot_digitizer`, `test_plot_digitizer_app`, `test_mcp_server`,
  `test_qucs_control`, `test_claude_code`, `test_pptx_doc` and `test_pen_reading` pass; ASan/UBSan (Debug):
  `test_pdf_viewer` passes with no report. The four lints are clean.
- **In the app** (offscreen, through the MCP server's screenshot): the pen is drawn right of Digitize, in the pen's
  colour with its menu's arrow, before Find. Not drawn on by hand in the running app on the display.
